Common questions

I lost my recovery phrase. Can you restore my wallet?

No, and we want to be honest rather than encouraging about this. BEAR Wallet is non-custodial: your keys are generated on your device and never sent to us. We hold nothing that could rebuild your wallet.

If you still have the wallet open on your device, go to Settings → Security → Recovery Phrase and reveal it now, then write it on paper and store it somewhere safe. Once the app is uninstalled or the device is lost, an unrecorded phrase is gone permanently.

What does “non-custodial” actually mean?

It means you hold your own funds. Your keys live in your phone’s secure storage, encrypted with your PIN. We never take possession of them, so we cannot freeze your account, reverse a payment, or lose your money in a company failure.

The trade-off is real: there is no support line that can undo a mistake. Sending to a wrong address, approving a malicious transaction, or losing your phrase are all permanent.

My token shows a zero balance, or the app says I have no trustline

On the XRP Ledger you must hold a trustline to an issuer before you can hold their token. If you have not set one up, the balance shows as zero.

Tokens with names longer than three characters are stored on the ledger in a hexadecimal form while being displayed to you in readable letters. If a balance looks wrong, please send us the token name, the issuer address, and a transaction hash, and we will look at the specific case.

Why do I need XRP just sitting in my account?

That is the ledger’s reserve requirement, not a BEAR fee, and we never receive it. The XRP Ledger requires a small XRP balance to keep an account active, plus a further small amount for each trustline, offer, or other object your account owns.

The reserve is set by the network and can change by validator vote. It is released back to you if you remove the object it was held against.

My transaction or swap failed

Common causes, roughly in order of likelihood:

  • The price moved. If the market shifted beyond your slippage tolerance, the ledger rejects the trade rather than filling it at a worse price. Raising slippage slightly and retrying usually works; the App will suggest this when it detects the cause.
  • Not enough XRP for the reserve or network fee. Every transaction needs a small amount of spare XRP.
  • No trustline for the token you are trying to receive.
  • Not enough liquidity for the size you tried to trade.

A failed transaction still consumes its small network fee — that is how the ledger prevents spam, and it does not go to us. If the reason is not clear, send us the transaction hash.

I am not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are enabled for BEAR Wallet in your phone’s system settings, and that the toggle is on in Settings in the App for the specific wallet you expect alerts for.

Alerts are per-wallet, so a newly added wallet needs its own toggle. Battery-optimisation and data-saver modes on some Android devices can also delay or suppress delivery.

How do I delete my data?

On your device: use Factory Reset in Settings, or uninstall the App. Both remove your keys and all local data. Make sure your recovery phrase is written down first if you intend to restore the wallet later.

Anything we hold: email support@bearpark.xyz and we will delete it.

On the blockchain: nobody can delete this, including us. The XRP Ledger is public and permanent by design. See the Privacy Policy.

I think I found a security vulnerability

Please tell us before telling anyone else. Email support@bearpark.xyz with “Security Disclosure” in the subject and enough detail to reproduce the issue.

We will acknowledge you, work on a fix, and credit you if you would like to be named. Please do not test against other people’s wallets or funds.
