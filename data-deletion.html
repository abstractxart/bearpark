Data Deletion Request

BEAR Wallet · Effective September 12, 2026 · Last updated September 12, 2026

The short version

BEAR Wallet has no accounts, no sign-up and no passwords. The one thing we store that is tied to you is your public XRPL wallet address.

Your recovery phrase and private keys were never ours — they are generated and stored on your device alone, so there is nothing on that front for us to delete.

This page is the deletion pathway for BEAR Wallet, published by BEAR Technologies LLC, a Colorado limited liability company (“BEAR”, “we”, “us”). For the full picture of what we collect and why, see the Privacy Policy.

1. What we hold

Our servers receive your public XRPL wallet address in order to look up your balances, tokens and transaction history. Our referral system records that address if you were referred by another user, or referred someone yourself. Alongside it we record the IP address and browser user agent from the moment you sign an authentication challenge or claim a referral.

If you turned on incoming-payment notifications in the mobile app, we also hold a push notification token. It is filed against a keyed hash of your address rather than the address itself, and the token is encrypted at rest.

That is the whole list.

2. What we never had

Your recovery phrase, private keys and PIN are generated and stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us. There is nothing for us to delete, and nothing we could return to you if you lost them.

To remove them, delete the wallet in the app or uninstall it — but make sure your recovery phrase is written down first, because that action is irreversible.

3. What cannot be deleted

Please read this one

Your activity on the XRP Ledger is public and permanent. Transactions, balances, trustlines and NFTs live on a decentralised blockchain that no single party controls.

Deleting our records removes them from our systems. It does not — and cannot — erase anything from the ledger itself, and anyone can still read that history using any public XRPL explorer.

4. How to request deletion

Email us from any mail client with your wallet address and we will action the request within 30 days, replying to confirm once it is done.

  • Send to: support@bearpark.xyz
  • Subject: Data deletion request — BEAR Wallet
  • Include: your XRPL wallet address — the one starting r

Open a pre-filled email — it opens your own mail app with the subject and message written for you. Paste your address where the template asks, and send.

Why email and not a form

A “delete this wallet’s records” button would need to prove you own the address before it could act — otherwise anyone could submit someone else’s. An unauthenticated endpoint that deletes data on request is a worse problem than the one it solves, so we keep a human in the loop. That is the right amount of friction for something irreversible.

5. What gets removed

  • Referral records associating your address with a referrer, or with users you referred.
  • Any cached profile or fee-tier records keyed to your address.
  • Your push notification token, if one is registered.
  • The session rows holding the IP address and browser user agent recorded when you signed an authentication challenge or claimed a referral.

Because the app is usable without an account, in most cases we hold nothing linked to you to begin with — and we will tell you plainly if that turns out to be the case for your address.

6. Removing everything on your device

Deletion above covers what sits on our servers. Everything the app keeps locally — encrypted keys, wallet labels, address book, preferences, cached icons — is erased by using Factory Reset in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
